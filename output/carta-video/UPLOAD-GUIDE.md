# CARTA — Play Store preview video

## Deliverables
- `CARTA-PlayStore-EN-1080p.mp4`: final 30-second landscape video, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264, stereo AAC.
- `thumbnail.png`: matching 1920 × 1080 YouTube thumbnail.
- `CARTA-English.srt`: optional English captions matching the on-screen message. There is no spoken voiceover.
- `preview.html`: local playback page.
- `../CARTA-Remotion-Source.zip`: editable Remotion project, local assets, fonts and original soundtrack generator. Dependencies can be restored with `npm ci`.

## Upload steps
1. Upload the MP4 to your YouTube channel.
2. Set visibility to **Public** or **Unlisted**. Enable embedding, avoid age restrictions, and turn off monetization for this preview.
3. Optionally set the supplied thumbnail and upload `CARTA-English.srt` as English captions.
4. Copy the direct YouTube video URL, without playlist or timestamp parameters.
5. In Play Console, open your English main store listing and paste the URL into the **Preview video** field. Keep your feature graphic from the existing image pack.

Google Play uses a YouTube URL for this field rather than a direct MP4 upload. The video may autoplay muted for up to 30 seconds, so the complete message is presented on screen.

Official guidance checked 13 September 2026: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en

## Edit and render
Extract the Remotion source archive into a folder, install Node.js, and run:
```
npm ci
npm run dev
```
Open the `Carta-PlayStore` composition. Its six scenes are separate files under `src/scenes`. All image and font assets are local under `public`. The timeline is 900 frames at 30 fps.

Render after edits:
```
npx remotion render src/index.ts Carta-PlayStore CARTA-PlayStore-EN-1080p.mp4 --codec=h264 --crf=16 --audio-bitrate=320k --pixel-format=yuv420p --image-format=png --concurrency=4
```

## Production record
- Visual identity: ivory, champagne gold and midnight blue; Playfair Display and Inter typography.
- Hook: “Some places stay with you. Turn yours into map art.”
- Product visuals: actual CARTA UI captures and artwork generated by CARTA from Paris OpenStreetMap road data, with animated presentation framing. These are animated captures, not a recording of live touch interaction.
- Soundtrack: an original 100 BPM ambient instrumental synthesized for this project, with soft keys, pads, pulse and transition accents. No third-party music samples or stock recordings were used.
- Main UI is shown from the opening scene through approximately 25 seconds. End card emphasizes the product's map artwork and name.
- The file has not been uploaded to YouTube or published in Play Console.
